# Why the HydraDG FCG was updated — 2026-08-19

The project FCG was expanded because the project now has additional material custody dependencies that directly affect what a judge sees:

1. downloaded datasets are now separate frozen source objects;
2. total atomization creates record-level derived custody objects;
3. SeedGraph admission and HydraDB projection are separate transformations;
4. website statements are derived presentation artifacts and need links back to evidence;
5. release-status labels can drift unless tied to one evidence-derived status object;
6. Vercel and static fallback are alternative delivery artifacts and must not be confused with experiment execution;
7. turn-level human/AI contributions need non-self-referential hashing and explicit signing state.

Without this update, the graph would stop at scientific results and lose custody over the transformation from those results into the website, demo and submission package.
