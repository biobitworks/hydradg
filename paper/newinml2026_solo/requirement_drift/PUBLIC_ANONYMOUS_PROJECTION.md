# Public Anonymous Projection (Requirement Drift)

Allowed generic statement for anonymous materials:

> Submission requirements were treated as versioned evidence rather than
> timeless constants; conflicting requirement observations remain preserved and
> superseding operational requirements are explicitly linked.

**Not admitted to anonymous bundle:**

- Organizer Discord content
- Organizer-identifying material
- Internal OpenReview operator transcriptions with portal metadata
- Private filesystem paths or repo-identifying URLs

See internal `NEWINML_REQUIREMENT_DRIFT_FCG.jsonl` for full case study.
